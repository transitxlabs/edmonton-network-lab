03 / A checkable calculation

Keep the assumptions. Rebuild the result.

Independent calculation

A compiled round-based engine is compared with a separately written connection scan on random queries. Fixtures also use a journey enumerator.

Exact deletion reuse

Deleting service shrinks feasible journeys. A selected optimum that survives certifies the same arrival. Affected batches are rerouted and checked against full calculations.

Geometry and timetable

Walking plus zero-wait geometry plus the signed timetable component equals scheduled access. Headway and span are combined because the extension rule creates artificial all-day service for short patterns.

04 / Boundaries of the result

Scheduled access, with stated limits.

  • Hourly samples cover one weekday; between-sample effects and other days may differ.
  • Road centrelines approximate walking. Crossings, barriers, entrances, mobility constraints and pathway times remain uncertain.
  • Census populations use one interior point per area. Points snap to the largest road component within 300 m. If this fails, the nearest point of the official polygon is used when its connector is within 300 m. Original-to-access straight distance adds walking time at 1.33 m/s on access and egress; original coordinates remain recorded. Stops without a connector serve only as on-board stops.
  • At 1.33 m/s, walking may overstate access for older people and in winter. Integer-ceiling times and omitted trails, walkways and footbridges understate reach. Roads without sidewalks, retained ramp and collector-distributor segments and straight links that ignore barriers may overstate reach. The City's segment-type field marks 820 ramp and collector-distributor segments (733 of them Arterial-Class B). They are retained as walkable, which may overstate reach near interchanges.
  • Libraries are selected destinations. Opening hours and capacity do not constrain this metric.
  • No demand, ridership, fare or capacity claims. Exact reuse refers to this frozen routing model.
  • The prospective real-time comparison awaits twenty eligible weekdays, capped at 45 complete service days. The pilot supports no reliability finding.

The 12 moved access points are 592 m to 4,329 m from their interior points, adding 446 s to 3,256 s (about 7 to 54 minutes) of walking. 12 areas (5,553 residents) moved their access point. The frozen 7 exceptions (2,780 residents) remain in every denominator with access computed as is. The build requires this exact failure set and rejects any different set.
